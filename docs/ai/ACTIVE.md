- Objective: Make Vietnamese the default application language and keep translated subtitle tracks preferred for online and downloaded playback; translate Chinese cues in 20-line chunks with four-cue context overlap, 60-second API timeout, retry, responsive playback prioritization, and persistent background completion.
- State: The subtitle translation repair and card-triggered background translation are implemented locally. The repair emits a zero-progress update immediately, explains when AI Translate is disabled, and rolls back to the original VTT if a generated VTT cannot be mounted by the player. Translation defaults to five direct workers plus exactly six workers for each working configured subtitle proxy. New installs default to Vietnamese with AI Translate enabled; common fixed UI labels use an offline Vietnamese dictionary and all other `uiTx()` text continues through the normal cache/AI translation path.
- Selection: AI target track, regular target track, then regular Chinese source, then AI Chinese source. Manual selection persists until the next part/video. AI-off uses available target tracks or complete local translation cache without new model calls.
- Translation: Queue prioritizes the chunk currently playing, then upcoming chunks, then already-viewed chunks; pending work is reordered on seek. The first completed chunk is emitted immediately and later partial VTT refreshes are limited to 400 ms. Checkpoints persist completed and failed chunk indexes by video/part/language/source content in app-support storage, independently of the temporary image cache. A detached video page leaves its shared translation session running; retries keep the visible partial VTT and back off 8→16→32→60 seconds. The completed session prefetches the next captured playlist item even after navigation. Size/concurrency settings are captured per video/part. The legacy in-memory full-VTT cache now retains 64 recent tracks (was 6); the durable translation checkpoints are not auto-evicted.
- Card translation: Standard horizontal and vertical video cards expose a `Dịch sub` cover action. It reports preparation/progress/retry state, becomes `Đã dịch` when done, and stores a small completed-video index alongside the durable translation cache. Opening that video reuses its source-hash-validated local subtitle cache.
- Progress UI: The subtitle button shows a live progress bar immediately at 0%, then reports processed cues, percentage, completion, or failed chunk count. When AI Translate is off, the subtitle menu explains that the Chinese source remains active; hard subtitle-load/startup failures show a single user-visible toast per video.
